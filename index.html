Description

This recipe is for a classic spaghetti bolognese, a hearty Italian pasta dish made with a rich tomato-based meat sauce. It's perfect for family dinners and easy to prepare!

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti pasta
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Sauté onions and garlic in olive oil until golden.
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Pour in tomato sauce and season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  5. Simmer sauce for 20 minutes.
  6. Drain spaghetti and mix with the sauce. Serve hot.
